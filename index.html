Mood Dude

Mood Dude runs on his own. You make him jump, and you change his mood: he can only walk on ground of his own colour, and he walks straight through walls that aren’t.

Change his mood, then tap the ground to jump.

The games

Three games, one dude. All of them are made by eiilo for Android phones and tablets.

Mood Dude

18 levels and a tutorial

The original. Every level is built by hand, from a gentle first run to sliding brick walls over long gaps. Collect a mood to unlock its colour, then switch between them to cross ground and walls.

  • Plays offline, with no account to create.
  • Tap to jump, tap a mood to switch.
  • Picks up at the last level you reached.
Privacy policy

Mood Dude: Infinity

Endless, and always faster

The track is built as you run and never ends. Moods are a stock now: every pickup adds one, every switch spends one, so each change counts. Pits, walls in chains, sliding bricks and seven kinds of creature, from slimes to saw blades you can’t stomp.

  • Beat your best score, then the leaderboard.
  • Ten trophies, through Google Play Games.
  • Music and sound made for the game.
Privacy policy

Mood Dude: Adventures

100 levels in 8 worlds

A full platform adventure. This time you steer: walk, run, climb, swim and stomp across eight island worlds, from Sunny Meadows to Mood Castle. Ground and walls in your mood’s colour are solid and the others are ghosts, so switch mid-run, even mid-air, to cross coloured bridges and land on the right platforms.

  • Three hidden mood stars and a checkpoint in every level.
  • A boss at the end of each world, hurt only in its own mood.
  • Skins and trails for the dude from the in-game store.
  • Sixteen trophies, through Google Play Games.
